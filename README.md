# qard

Specification for a Shari`a-compliant community finance platform modelled on SoLo Funds and LaunchGood — a crowd-lending qard hasan marketplace where one or more lenders fund each borrower and repayments are distributed pro rata, with no tips, cost-based fees, a tabarru' protection fund, a zakat relief channel, and a later goods-murabaha module.

- [Specification](docs/spec.md) — working draft for Shariah board and legal review (October 2026)
- [Business plan](docs/business-plan.md) — Q4 2026 launch with one operator and Grok Bot agent teams, Stearns Bank (Stearns Salaam) as target bank partner, bootstrapped
- [Brief to US counsel](docs/brief-counsel.md) — licensing, payments and securities questions
- [Brief to the Shari'a Board](docs/brief-shariah-board.md) — charter, scope and questions for fatwa

Straightway Ethical Advisory (straightwayethical.com) is the platform's Shari'a Advisor and will provide the Shari'a Board. Sh. Taha Abdul-Basser is the executive liaison for the Shari'a Board and answers the board's questions during this stage of development.
